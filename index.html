The Experience

How a Farmer Uses Kisanframe

A simple, guided journey — from campaign click to a shareable, personalised AI-generated image, in under a minute.

📩
1. Farmer Receives the Campaign
Bayer's marketing team sends a campaign link to farmers via SMS, WhatsApp, or email. The farmer taps the link and lands directly on Kisanframe.
📸
2. Farmer Uploads Their Photo
The farmer simply uploads a photo of themselves from their phone. The relevant campaign is auto-selected based on the link, or the farmer can pick one manually.
🤖
3. AI Generates Their Personalised Image
Kisanframe's AI engine places the farmer's photo into the pre-built campaign template — designed and uploaded by Bayer's team in the admin console — producing a personalised image in seconds.
📥
4. Farmer Downloads & Shares
The farmer downloads their personalised creative or shares it directly to WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram — turning every farmer into a campaign amplifier for Bayer.
